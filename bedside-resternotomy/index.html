5 West · CTICU · SCU 10-214

Bedside Resternotomy

When a fresh cardiac surgery patient arrests or slides toward it, the chest may get opened in the room. Pick what the monitor is showing, make the calls, then work through what opening the chest takes: roles, prep, drape, Bovie, fire, instruments and internal defib.
