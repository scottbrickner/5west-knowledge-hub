Sedation practice · CP 3-162

SAT screen and RASS

Every morning, each ventilated patient gets an SAT safety screen from the RN. Sedatives come off for the SAT. Analgesics stay on at the lowest effective dose unless a provider orders otherwise.

SAT safety screen · any check = do not proceed
No exclusions checked. Proceed with SAT: turn off sedatives.
During the SAT
  1. Record RASS, neuro status and hemodynamics before stopping sedation, at 15 min, then every 30 min and PRN.
  2. Patient must be awake and follow at least one command (e.g., hold up two fingers) to pass.
  3. Fail if: RASS > +2 for ≥ 5 min · SpO₂ < 88% for ≥ 5 min · RR > 35 for ≥ 5 min · new arrhythmia · ICP ≥ 20 mmHg · 2 or more signs of respiratory distress (accessory muscles, abdominal paradox, diaphoresis, dyspnea).
  4. On failure: restart sedation at half the prior dose, titrate to the ordered RASS goal, and reattempt tomorrow.
  5. On pass: hand off to the RCP for the SBT screen immediately.
